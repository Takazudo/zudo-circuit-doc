The core ideas behind zudo-circuit-doc: component evidence, the config contract, the
generated-project layout, and how they fit together. Everything here describes **implemented**
behavior of `@takazudo/zudo-circuit-doc` — where the product differs from an earlier plan, this
site follows the code.

## In this section

- [The evidence contract](./evidence-contract.mdx) — files, facts and classes, verdicts,
  provenance, availability vs. authority, page index vs. label, calculations and their limits, and
  why coverage is not sign-off.
- [Publication](./publication.mdx) — the three gates, the selection lock, the assets allowlist,
  and why `assetViewerExclude` is not privacy.
- [Ownership and upgrades](./ownership-and-upgrades.mdx) — what belongs to the package, the
  project, or the generator; why there is no `update` command; and the scaffold-refresh procedure.
- [Inventory profiles](./inventory-profiles.mdx) — the manual (generic-v1) profile and the
  led-generator-v1 profile.
- [CAD fidelity](./cad-fidelity.mdx) — the four fidelity classes and what each one does, and does
  not, prove.
