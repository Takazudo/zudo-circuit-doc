- [Temperature sensor candidates](./tmp1075-candidate.mdx) compares TMP1075DR with TMP75, TMP117 and TMP102 for the example breakout.

To start one, copy `circuit/templates/project-docs/research/component-candidate.mdx` into this directory with a topic-specific filename, title and `sidebar_position`, and fill it as the research happens. A research page explains the question, the criteria and what the evidence establishes; exact identity, sources and facts go into the component evidence records. Candidates stay out of the inventory until one is selected.
